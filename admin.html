Publish your products to the live site

When you add products here, they are saved on this device. To update your live website, download the updated index.html and upload it to GitHub — Vercel redeploys in 30 seconds.

How to publish (3 steps):
1. Tap Download updated index.html above
2. Go to your GitHub repo → index.html → tap ✏️ pencil → then tap the upload icon
3. Upload the downloaded file → Commit changes

Vercel detects the change and redeploys your live site in ~30 seconds. ✅
